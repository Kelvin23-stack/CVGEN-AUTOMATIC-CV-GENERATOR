About CVGEN

A CV builder that lives entirely in your browser

CVGEN is a free tool for turning your work history into a polished, professional CV — no design software, no formatting headaches, and no data leaving your device.

Enter your personal details, experience, education, skills and more into a guided form, and watch a real, print-ready CV take shape beside it in real time. Pick from several professionally designed templates, then export the result as a PDF you can send straight to employers.

It's built for students, graduates and job seekers who need something that looks professional fast — whether that's for a first internship application or a career change. Everything you create is saved locally in your own browser, so your CV is always private and always yours.
